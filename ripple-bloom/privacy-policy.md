# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy is for **Ripple Bloom**, a puzzle game for Android made by Rosi Tech Labs.

## The short version

- **Ripple Bloom has no account, no sign-in and no ads.** You never type your name, email or anything personal into the game.
- **While Ripple Bloom is being tested, it sends anonymous play statistics** (which ponds you play, where you tap, how many drops you use, which buttons you tap, your phone model and how smoothly the game runs on it) so we can make the puzzles fairer and fix problems. They are linked to a random 5-letter code made on your phone, never to you.
- **You can turn the statistics off** at any time: Settings → Send test stats.
- **You can ask us to delete them:** email us the tester code shown in Settings.
- We never sell your data and never share it with advertisers.

## Who we are

Ripple Bloom is made by Rosi Tech Labs, an independent app developer ("we", "us"). Contact: [rositech104@gmail.com](mailto:rositech104@gmail.com).

## What the game keeps on your phone

Your progress (ponds played, lotuses, coins, boosters, lantern designs, the daily gift and streak), your settings, and the times of day you opened the game in the last four weeks (used only to choose the time of the daily reminder, see below). It stays in the game's private storage on your phone.

Android may also back up your progress to your own Google account, so it comes back on a new phone. That copy is handled by Google under its privacy policy; we never see it. You can switch it off in your phone's backup settings.

## What the game sends to us (test statistics)

While the game is in testing, it sends small play events over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to a Google Sheet that only we can open:

- **A tester code:** 5 random letters made on your phone the first time you open the game. It is shown at the bottom of Settings. It links the events of one installation; reinstalling the game makes a new one. It is not your name, your phone number, your Google account, your advertising ID or your phone's hardware ID.
- **Play events:** which pond you played, where you tapped on the pond and the time between taps, how many drops you used, whether you won, how long a try took, which help you used (hints, boosters, extra drops, test ads), coins earned and spent, buttons you tapped on menus, settings you changed, whether you allowed notifications, the daily reminder's time and whether it was shown, whether you opened the game from the reminder, the screen you were on when you left the game, and the day number since you installed the game.
- **About your phone:** phone model, Android version, screen size and refresh rate, memory size, number of processor cores, graphics chip, the language and country chosen in your phone's settings, your time zone, and the game's version.
- **How smoothly the game runs:** frames per second, how many frames were slow, how long the game took to start, and how much memory it used.
- **Time:** your phone's local time of each event.

We do **not** collect your name, email, contacts, photos, location (the country above is your phone's language setting, not where you are), microphone, camera, advertising ID or any other identifier from your phone. The game has no text boxes, so nothing you type is sent.

**Why:** to see where puzzles are too hard or too easy, where players get confused, whether the game runs well on cheaper phones, which help people find and use, whether people come back the next day, and to find problems. We look at totals and at one tester code at a time, never to identify a person.

**Where it goes:** the events are stored in a Google Sheet in our Google account. Google provides the storage and the small script that receives them, under Google's own terms. Nobody else receives them.

**How long we keep them:** until the testing ends and the numbers have been studied, and never longer than 12 months after they were sent. Then they are deleted.

**Turning it off:** Settings → Send test stats. When it is off, nothing is sent and anything waiting on your phone is deleted.

## Deleting your data

- **On your phone:** uninstalling the game deletes everything it kept.
- **Statistics we received:** email your tester code (Settings, bottom line) to [rositech104@gmail.com](mailto:rositech104@gmail.com) and we will delete every row with that code within 30 days.

## The daily reminder

The game shows one gentle reminder a day, on by default. Android asks you first whether the game may show notifications, and you can turn the reminder off in Settings (or in your phone's settings). Its time follows when you usually play: the game looks at the times of day you opened it in the last four weeks, on your phone. The reminder is scheduled and shown by your phone (and set again after the phone restarts); nothing is sent from a server to show it. It does not appear on a day you already played.

## Sending feedback

"Send feedback" in Settings opens your own email app with our address and your tester code filled in, so we can match your message to the statistics. You choose whether to send it. We use your email address only to reply to you.

## Sharing a result

The Share button opens your phone's own share panel with a short text about your result. You choose where it goes; the game does not see what you do there.

## Children

Ripple Bloom is made for people aged 13 and over, not for children, and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Coins and test ads

Coins are earned by playing and spent only inside the game. There are no purchases. The "test ad" screens are placeholders: no ad is loaded and no advertising company receives anything.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date. If a change means the game collects more, we will update this page, the game and Google Play's data safety section first.
